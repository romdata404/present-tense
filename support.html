Subscriptions

What is free?

The quiz and three preview lines from your pack. Everything else needs a subscription: annual at $39.99 per year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or weekly at $4.99 per week. These are US prices; the app shows the price for your country before you confirm.

How do I cancel on an iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Present tense and tap Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Apple's instructions

How do I cancel on Android?

Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Choose Present tense and tap Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Google's instructions

Does deleting the app cancel my subscription?

No. Subscriptions belong to your Apple or Google account, so cancel them there as described above.

How do I avoid a charge after the free trial?

Cancel at least 24 hours before the 7-day trial ends. If you don't cancel, the trial turns into the annual plan and you are charged $39.99 per year, or the price for your country shown in the app.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. For the App Store, go to reportaproblem.apple.com (how it works). For Google Play, see Google Play's refund policies. We can't issue App Store refunds ourselves.

I paid, but the app still asks me to subscribe

Tap Restore purchases in the app, and check that your phone is signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the Support ID from Settings, then Subscription, or the order number from your receipt.

Using the app

My reminders are not showing up

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they depend on your phone's notification settings.

  • iPhone: open Settings, then Notifications, then Present tense, and turn on Allow notifications. Check that a Focus is not hiding them.
  • Android: open Settings, then Apps, then Present tense, then Notifications, and turn them on. Battery saver settings on some phones can delay reminders.

I got a new phone

Your subscription comes with you: install the app, sign in to the same Apple Account or Google account, and tap Restore purchases. Your favourites, your own affirmations and your streak live only on your old phone. They may come across if you set up the new phone from a full backup of the old one, but we can't move them for you because we never have a copy.

Is Present tense therapy?

No. It is a wellness app with affirmations for everyday life. It is not medical advice, therapy or treatment, and it does not diagnose anything. If you are struggling, please talk to a qualified professional.

Your data

What do you know about me?

Very little. Your quiz answers, name, favourites, your own affirmations, reminder times and streak stay on your phone. Our subscription service, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous ID, your purchase status, basic device information and your IP address. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove everything it stores on your phone. To delete the purchase record RevenueCat holds for us, email us with the Support ID from Settings, or the order number from your Apple or Google receipt. Deleting that record doesn't cancel your subscription.
