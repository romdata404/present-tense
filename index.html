How it works

  1. Take the short quiz

    It picks your pack, your voice, your reminder times and the first name your lines can use.

  2. Get your lines through the day

    A few lines in the morning, at midday and in the evening, with reminders on your lock screen.

  3. Keep what lands

    Save favourites, write your own affirmations, share a card sized for stories, and check in each day.

Four voices, thirty days

Voices

Gentle, direct, hype or spiritual. The same situation, said the way you would say it to yourself.

A 30-day arc

  1. LandingDays 1 to 10
  2. BuildingDays 11 to 20
  3. OwningDays 21 to 30

What is free and what is paid

The quiz and three preview lines from your pack are free. Everything else needs a subscription, bought through the App Store or Google Play:

  • Annual: $39.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. After the trial you are charged $39.99 per year unless you cancel.
  • Weekly: $4.99 per week.

These are US prices. The app shows the price for your country before you confirm. A subscription renews automatically until you cancel it, and you can cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play settings. Read the subscription terms

No account, no ads, no tracking

Your quiz answers, first name, favourites, your own affirmations, reminder times and streak stay on your phone, and your reminders are scheduled there too. Apple or Google handle payment, and our subscription service, RevenueCat, checks whether your subscription is active. Read the privacy policy

Present tense is a wellness app, not medical advice, therapy or treatment. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency services.
